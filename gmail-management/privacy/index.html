Privacy policy

For gmail management. Last updated: 2026-11-14

What it reads

gmail management reads Gmail messages through the Gmail API: the sender, subject, preview (snippet) and labels of each message. It reads only from its owner's own Gmail account.

Why

It uses this information only to label, star, archive or trash the owner's own mail.

Where the data goes

The data is processed and stored only on the owner's own computer. It is not sold, not shared with anyone, not used for advertising, and not used to train general AI models.

The use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

How to stop or delete

You can revoke the app's access to your Google account at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions. Locally stored data is deleted on request.
